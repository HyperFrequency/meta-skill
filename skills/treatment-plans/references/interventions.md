# Evidence-Based Interventions

First- and second-line options with concrete dosing, plus non-pharmacological,
behavioral, and procedural modalities, and a documentation template. Confirm all
dosing against current primary guidelines before use — these are representative
starting points, not a formulary.

## Evidence grading

- **Levels:** I (systematic reviews/meta-analyses of RCTs, large RCTs) > II
  (individual RCTs, systematic reviews of observational studies) > III (cohort,
  case-control) > IV (case series/reports, expert opinion).
- **Recommendation strength:** Grade A (strong, high-quality) → B (moderate) → C
  (weak) → D (recommend against — harm or no benefit).

## Pharmacological — principles

Prescribe evidence-based agents for the indication; individualize for comorbidities,
organ function (renal/hepatic), allergies, interactions, age/pregnancy,
pharmacogenomics, and cost. Start low and titrate to target by response and
tolerance; monitor for adverse effects; avoid potentially inappropriate meds (Beers
Criteria in elderly); reduce polypharmacy where possible.

### First-line classes by indication

**Hypertension** (JNC-8, ACC/AHA): ACE inhibitors (lisinopril, enalapril — preferred
with diabetes/CKD/HF); ARBs (losartan, valsartan) if ACE-intolerant; CCBs
(amlodipine — effective in elderly, Black patients); thiazides (chlorthalidone,
HCTZ). Start one agent low, titrate to max tolerated before adding a second; 2-3
agents often needed; reassess every 2-4 weeks.

**Type 2 diabetes** (ADA): metformin first-line unless eGFR <30 (start 500-850 mg
daily/BID → up to 2000 mg/day). Second-line by comorbidity: SGLT2 inhibitors
(empagliflozin, dapagliflozin) if HF or CKD; GLP-1 agonists (semaglutide,
dulaglutide) if ASCVD or weight loss needed; DPP-4 inhibitors (sitagliptin) for low
hypoglycemia risk; sulfonylureas (glipizide, cheap but hypoglycemia risk); insulin
if HbA1c very high (>10%) or symptomatic.

**Depression** (APA): SSRIs first-line (sertraline, escitalopram, fluoxetine,
citalopram, paroxetine) — start low, titrate at 2-4 weeks, full 6-8 week trial,
continue 6-12 months after remission. Second-line SNRIs (venlafaxine, duloxetine —
esp. with chronic pain), bupropion (sexual side-effect concern, smoking cessation),
mirtazapine (insomnia/appetite). Augment partial response with a second
antidepressant, atypical antipsychotic (aripiprazole, quetiapine), or lithium/T3.

**Chronic pain** (WHO ladder, CDC): non-opioids first — acetaminophen 3-4 g/day
(normal liver), NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen, meloxicam — watch renal/BP/GI).
Neuropathic adjuvants: gabapentin 300 mg → 1800-3600 mg/day TID; pregabalin 75 mg
BID → 150-300 mg BID; duloxetine 60 mg/day; TCAs (amitriptyline, nortriptyline)
10-75 mg QHS (second-line). Topicals: lidocaine 5% patch, diclofenac gel, capsaicin.
Opioids only after multimodal therapy fails — lowest effective dose, short-acting,
avoid >90 MME/day; UDS, PDMP, naloxone; reassess and taper if functional goals unmet.

**HFrEF — foundational four (GDMT):** (1) ACE/ARB/ARNI — ARNI sacubitril/valsartan
24/26 → 97/103 mg BID (superior to ACE/ARB); (2) beta-blocker — carvedilol 3.125 →
25 mg BID, metoprolol succinate 12.5-25 → 200 mg/day, or bisoprolol 1.25 → 10
mg/day; (3) MRA — spironolactone 12.5-25 mg (watch K+); (4) SGLT2 inhibitor —
dapagliflozin or empagliflozin 10 mg/day. Add loop diuretic for volume (no mortality
benefit); hydralazine-isosorbide (esp. African-American or ACE/ARB-intolerant).

## Non-pharmacological

**Diet:** Mediterranean (CVD prevention, diabetes; ~30% CV-event reduction in
PREDIMED) — high produce/whole grains/legumes/nuts/olive oil, moderate fish/poultry,
low red meat/sweets. DASH (hypertension; SBP −8 to −14 mmHg) — produce, low-fat
dairy, sodium <2300 (ideally <1500) mg. Carb counting for diabetes (45-60 g/meal).
Weight management: 500-750 kcal/day deficit → 1-2 lb/week; structured programs beat
self-directed; consider pharmacotherapy or bariatric surgery at BMI ≥30-35 with
comorbidities.

**Exercise:** 150 min/week moderate (or 75 vigorous) aerobic + resistance training
2-3x/week all major groups + balance/flexibility (fall prevention). Prescribe by the
FITT principle (frequency, intensity, time, type); cardiac clearance if indicated.

**Smoking cessation** (5 A's: ask, advise, assess, assist, arrange): pharmacotherapy
doubles quit rates — NRT (patch/gum/lozenge), varenicline (most effective, start 1
week pre-quit), bupropion; combinations work best; add counseling
(1-800-QUIT-NOW). Set a quit date within 30 days, follow up within a week.

**Sleep hygiene / CBT-I** for insomnia; **stress management** (mindfulness,
progressive muscle relaxation, diaphragmatic breathing, yoga/tai chi).

## Behavioral interventions

- **CBT** — first-line for anxiety and insomnia, equivalent to antidepressants for
  mild-moderate depression; 12-16 weekly 50-min sessions with homework. Variants:
  CBT-I (insomnia), CBT-CP (chronic pain — pain education, activity pacing,
  de-catastrophizing).
- **Motivational interviewing** — for ambivalence about behavior change (diet,
  exercise, substance use, adherence): express empathy, develop discrepancy, roll
  with resistance, support self-efficacy; elicit "change talk."
- **Self-management education** — improves diabetes, asthma, HF, and chronic-pain
  outcomes; teach self-monitoring, medication management, symptom action plans.

## Procedural and interventional

- **Rehabilitation therapies** — PT (therapeutic exercise, manual therapy, gait/
  balance, modalities as adjuncts only), OT (ADL training, adaptive equipment,
  cognitive retraining), SLP (dysphagia, aphasia, dysarthria). Prescribe frequency,
  duration, and specific goals.
- **Interventional pain** — epidural steroid injection (radicular pain, short-term
  relief); facet injections → radiofrequency ablation (6-12 mo relief for facet
  pain); spinal cord stimulation (refractory neuropathic pain, FBSS/CRPS; 50-60%
  achieve ≥50% relief; trial before permanent implant); intrathecal pump (severe
  refractory/cancer pain).
- **Surgery** — refer after adequate conservative trial (typically ≥6-12 weeks), for
  progressive neuro deficit, cauda equina (emergency), severe functional limitation,
  or correctable structural pathology; use shared decision-making.

## Integrative therapies

Acupuncture (moderate evidence: chronic LBP, knee OA, tension/migraine headache);
massage (modest: chronic LBP, anxiety); yoga (back pain, balance, stress); MBSR
(8-week program; stress, anxiety, chronic pain); chiropractic (acute/chronic LBP,
neck pain — avoid high-velocity manipulation with osteoporosis/instability).

## Per-intervention documentation template

For each intervention, record: **name/type**, **indication** (why this, this
patient), **evidence** (guideline/RCT support), **dose/frequency/duration**,
**expected benefit** (what improves, how much, by when), **monitoring** (how response
is assessed), **risks/side effects**, and **alternatives considered**.

Example: "Lisinopril 10 mg PO daily — ACE inhibitor for HTN and renal protection in
diabetes. Titrate to 20 mg in 2-4 weeks if BP not at goal and tolerated (monitor
cough, K+). Target BP <130/80."
