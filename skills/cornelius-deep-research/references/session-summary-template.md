## Quality Standards

**Topic selection (autonomous):** strategic alignment with existing strengths or
critical gaps; cross-domain potential bridging multiple hubs; empirical grounding
(active 2024-2025 research); practical relevance.

**Research:** recency (prioritize 2024-2025); rigor (peer-reviewed/reputable);
depth (15-25 major papers minimum); breadth (multiple perspectives); empirics
(quantified results, metrics).

**Insight extraction:** novelty (only genuinely new perspectives); dedupe ALWAYS
before creating; full citations; link to existing KB; quality over quantity
(15-25 high-value insights, not 100 mediocre).

**Connection discovery:** prefer non-obvious links; cross-domain consilience zones
are gold; synthesis orientation (article/framework opportunities); actionable
concrete next steps.

**Documentation:** comprehensive changelogs per phase; clear file organization
(session folders in Document Insights); keep master `CHANGELOG.md` current;
maintain an audit trail future-you can follow.
