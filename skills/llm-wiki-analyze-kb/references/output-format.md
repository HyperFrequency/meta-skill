# knowledge-base-analysis.md — Output Format

The output is a single condensed document that reveals the vault's cognitive fingerprint and
growth opportunities without drowning the reader in detail. Overwrite the existing file; do
not append.

## Required sections

1. **Executive summary** — key statistics (note count, edge count, density, component count,
   most-connected clusters) in a few lines.
2. **Structure map** — the vault's organizational layers (permanent notes, MOCs/maps of
   content, sources, meta) and how notes flow between them. Condensed.
3. **Thematic hubs** — the major clusters. For each: its core thesis in one line plus its
   key hub notes only. Do not enumerate every note.
4. **Network properties** — connectivity metrics and the critical bridge notes that link
   otherwise-separate clusters.
5. **Content topology & sources** — where the ideas come from; top-tier influences only.
6. **Maturity assessment** — the lifecycle/layer distribution and the current active focus.
7. **Recommendations** — high-priority enhancement opportunities only (weak links, orphaned
   clusters, under-developed hubs).

## Length & style budget (hard constraints)

- **Length:** 600–800 lines maximum — roughly half the size of a verbose first draft.
- **Prioritize:** core insights, actionable frameworks, critical statistics, key bridges.
- **Eliminate:** redundant examples, repeated explanations, verbose elaboration.
- **Focus:** what is unique, what is actionable, what is growing, what is next.
- **Style:** bullet points over paragraphs; tables where they compress well.
- **No duplication:** say each thing once, clearly, then move on.
