# ISO 13485:2016 Requirements by Clause

A plain-language walkthrough of the normative clauses (4-8). Clauses 1-3 (scope,
normative references, terms) are informational and carry no QMS obligations. Restate
these in your own words when drafting; do not paste standard text into your documents.

---

## Clause 4 — Quality Management System

### 4.1 General requirements
- Establish, document, implement, and **maintain** a QMS and keep it effective.
- Identify the QMS processes, their sequence and interaction, the criteria/methods to
  control them, and the resources to run and monitor them (process approach).
- **Outsourced processes** (e.g. sterilization, calibration) must be controlled and that
  control documented — outsourcing never transfers responsibility.
- **4.1.5 Risk management**: document risk-management requirements across product
  realization and keep records (methodology aligns with ISO 14971).
- **4.1.6 Software validation**: validate QMS software applications before first use and
  after changes, proportionate to risk; keep validation records.

### 4.2 Documentation requirements
- **4.2.1** QMS documentation = quality policy + objectives, Quality Manual, the
  procedures/records ISO 13485 requires, documents the organization needs for effective
  processes, and Medical Device Files required by regulation.
- **4.2.2 Quality Manual** must contain: QMS scope (with justified exclusions), the
  documented procedures or references to them, a description of process interactions, and
  the documentation structure.
- **4.2.3 Medical Device File** (per device type/family): general description & intended
  use; label/IFU specs; product and manufacturing specs; procedures for purchasing,
  manufacturing, servicing; measuring/monitoring procedures; installation requirements
  (if applicable); risk management file; verification & validation info; design &
  development file when applicable.
- **4.2.4 Control of documents** — documented procedure to approve before issue; review,
  update, re-approve; identify changes and current revision status; make current versions
  available at point of use; keep documents legible/identifiable; control external
  documents; prevent unintended use of obsolete documents (and identify any retained).
- **4.2.5 Control of records** — documented procedure for identification, storage,
  security, integrity, retrieval, retention, and disposition. Retention ≥ device
  lifetime as the organization defines it, and ≥ any applicable regulatory minimum.

## Clause 5 — Management Responsibility

- **5.1 Commitment** — top management evidences commitment: communicate the importance of
  regulatory/customer requirements, set the quality policy and objectives, run management
  reviews, and ensure resources.
- **5.2 Customer focus** — determine and meet customer and regulatory requirements.
- **5.3 Quality policy** — appropriate to the organization, commits to meeting
  requirements and maintaining QMS effectiveness, frames the objectives, is communicated
  and understood, and is reviewed for continuing suitability.
- **5.4 Planning** — measurable quality objectives at relevant functions/levels,
  consistent with the policy; maintain QMS integrity through change.
- **5.5 Responsibility, authority, communication** — define/communicate roles; appoint a
  **management representative** who ensures processes are maintained and reports QMS
  performance to top management; establish internal communication on QMS effectiveness.
- **5.6 Management review** — review the QMS at planned intervals (at least annually).
  **Inputs**: audit results, feedback, process/product conformity, CAPA status, prior
  follow-ups, changes affecting the QMS, improvement recommendations, new/revised
  regulatory requirements. **Outputs**: decisions on QMS and product improvements,
  resource needs, and changes to maintain effectiveness. Keep records.

---

## Clause 6 — Resource Management

- **6.1** Provide resources to run/maintain the QMS and meet requirements.
- **6.2 Human resources** — personnel affecting quality must be competent (education,
  training, skills, experience); identify competence needs, provide and evaluate
  training, and keep competence records.
- **6.3 Infrastructure** — provide and maintain buildings, workspace, utilities, process
  equipment (hardware/software), and supporting services; document maintenance where it
  can affect product quality and keep maintenance records.
- **6.4 Work environment & contamination control** — manage and document the work
  environment where it affects conformity; where applicable, document contamination
  control and special arrangements for contaminated/potentially contaminated product.

## Clause 7 — Product Realization

- **7.1 Planning** — plan realization processes: quality objectives, needed
  documentation/resources, verification/validation/monitoring/measurement/inspection,
  handling/storage/distribution/traceability, and conformity records. Document risk
  management throughout and keep records.
- **7.2 Customer-related processes** — determine customer, implied, regulatory, and
  organization-added requirements; review before commitment and resolve differences;
  document arrangements for customer communication (product info; enquiry/order handling;
  feedback including complaints; advisory notices).
- **7.3 Design & development** (excludable only for organizations that do no design):
  - **7.3.2** plan the stages, reviews, verification, validation, responsibilities.
  - **7.3.3 inputs** — functional/performance/usability/safety requirements, regulatory
    requirements & standards, risk-management outputs, prior-design info; review for
    adequacy and resolve conflicts.
  - **7.3.4 outputs** — meet inputs; inform purchasing/production/service; contain or
    reference acceptance criteria; specify characteristics essential for safe use.
  - **7.3.5-7.3.7** systematic reviews, verification, and validation per plan; validate
    before delivery under defined conditions; keep records.
  - **7.3.8 transfer** to manufacturing (verify manufacturing output meets design output);
    **7.3.9 change control**; **7.3.10 design & development files**.
- **7.4 Purchasing** — evaluate/select/monitor suppliers on ability to meet requirements;
  purchasing information specifies product/process/personnel/QMS requirements and change
  notification; verify purchased product (extent + release method documented).
- **7.5 Production & service provision**:
  - **7.5.1** controlled conditions: procedures/work instructions, suitable
    infrastructure and equipment, monitoring/measuring, release/delivery/post-delivery,
    labelling and packaging, servicing procedures if applicable.
  - **7.5.2** cleanliness of product (if applicable); **7.5.3** installation (if
    applicable); **7.5.4** servicing (if a specified requirement); **7.5.5/7.5.7**
    sterile-device records and sterilization/sterile-barrier validation.
  - **7.5.6 process validation** where output cannot be verified by later monitoring:
    review/approval criteria, equipment/personnel qualification, methods, records,
    revalidation criteria, and validation of production software.
  - **7.5.8 identification**; **7.5.9 traceability** (extent defined; distribution/
    consignee/quantity records for the required period); **7.5.10 customer property**;
    **7.5.11 preservation** (identification, handling, packaging, storage, protection).
- **7.6 Control of monitoring & measuring equipment** — determine the measurements and
  equipment; calibrate/verify at intervals against traceable standards; identify
  calibration status; safeguard from invalidating adjustment and damage; assess prior
  results if found out of tolerance; confirm measurement software; keep records.

## Clause 8 — Measurement, Analysis and Improvement

- **8.1** Plan monitoring/measurement/analysis/improvement to show product and QMS
  conformity and maintain effectiveness, including statistical techniques where relevant.
- **8.2 Monitoring and measurement**:
  - **8.2.1 feedback** — documented procedure with an early-warning system gathering
    post-production information, feeding risk management and CAPA.
  - **8.2.2 complaint handling** — timely receipt, recording, evaluation, investigation;
    responsibilities for regulatory reporting; inform the customer; capture complaints
    handled elsewhere; keep records.
  - **8.2.3 reporting to regulatory authorities** — notifications and advisory notices per
    applicable requirements; keep records.
  - **8.2.4 internal audit** — planned program by process importance/changes/prior
    results; defined criteria/scope/frequency/methods; objective, impartial auditors;
    follow-up to verify effectiveness; keep records.
  - **8.2.5 / 8.2.6** monitor processes and product; record release authority; do not
    release until planned arrangements are complete.
- **8.3 Control of nonconforming product** — identify, document, evaluate, segregate, and
  disposition; handle nonconformity detected **before** delivery (correct, concession per
  regulation, preclude original use) and **after** delivery (action + regulatory
  notification); control **rework** (evaluate effects, approve, re-verify); keep records.
- **8.4 Analysis of data** — determine, collect, analyze data on customer satisfaction,
  product conformity, process/product trends (preventive-action opportunities),
  suppliers, and other inputs including feedback and risk-management outputs; keep records.
- **8.5 Improvement**:
  - **8.5.1** identify and implement changes maintaining effectiveness using policy,
    objectives, audits, data analysis, CAPA, and management review.
  - **8.5.2 corrective action** — review nonconformities/complaints, determine causes,
    evaluate need, plan/implement/document actions, review effectiveness; keep records.
  - **8.5.3 preventive action** — determine potential nonconformities and causes,
    evaluate need, plan/implement/document actions, review effectiveness; keep records.

## Key regulatory update — FDA QMSR harmonization

Effective **2 February 2026**, FDA 21 CFR Part 820 is harmonized with ISO 13485:2016 and
renamed the **Quality Management System Regulation (QMSR)**. The unified **Medical Device
File** supersedes the separate DHF (Design History File), DMR (Device Master Record), and
DHR (Device History Record). Plan documentation toward the consolidated MDF structure.
